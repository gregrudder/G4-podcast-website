Leather’d is an MMA podcast with Paul Craig and Ross Cooper. It has been recorded with G4 in Wishaw. Paul Craig is a retired UFC fighter. Permission has been given to name the show and both hosts.

The same show was also filmed on location at Higher Level MMA with Ross Cooper, Sean Clancy Jnr and Paul Craig, for technique breakdowns for the Leather’d Patreon. Those photographs are on this page, with the Wishaw studio photographs.
