Leather’d is an MMA podcast with Paul Craig and Ross Cooper. It has been recorded with G4 in Wishaw. Permission has been given to name the show and both hosts.

The show’s own site describes it as a Scottish MMA podcast and credits an association with the wider G4 name. The public podcast listing has also credited G4 Podcast Studio on episodes. This page stays with that: it is an MMA show, recorded with the studio, with those two people.

[leatherd.co.uk](https://leatherd.co.uk/)

There are no download numbers, rankings or “results” on this page. We are not publishing them.

An MMA conversation is a useful reference for the room itself. Two or more people, specialist subject matter, enough space to talk without a corporate set. The Wishaw studios have couch and desk options, multi-camera recording, professional lighting, and a TV that can take a show’s own branding. Which of those was used on a particular Leather’d episode is not itemised here.

Studio time is booked by the hour. The rates are on the [pricing page](/pricing). If you want the episode cut, or short clips taken from it, ask for a quote with the booking. That work is not assumed from the hourly tier.
