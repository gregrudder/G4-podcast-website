A Pint and Two Shots is a podcast with Grado, Stephen Purdon and Chris Toal. It has been recorded with G4 in Wishaw. Permission has been given to name the show and the three of them on this site.

The Daily Record reported the programme as a Scottish football podcast and reported that the hosts would record it at the Wishaw studio. That article is the newspaper’s account:

[Scottish TV stars to exclusively use Wishaw studio for their new podcast](https://www.dailyrecord.co.uk/in-your-area/lanarkshire/scottish-tv-stars-exclusively-use-27650370)

This page does not repeat the article, and it does not add audience figures, episode counts or a claim about what the show did for anyone’s business. Those numbers are not published here.

What the booking shows is narrower, and it is the useful part if you are deciding whether the room can hold a conversation show. Three people, a hosted format, recorded in Wishaw rather than in a Glasgow city-centre suite. The studio has couch and desk sets and is set up for multi-camera recording. The exact set and camera count for any one episode of this show are not listed here, because they are not something we are publishing as a result.
