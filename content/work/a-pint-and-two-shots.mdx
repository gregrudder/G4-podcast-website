A Pint and Two Shots is Greg's sports and chat podcast, produced by G4 and recorded in the Wishaw studio. Permission has been given to name the show. The hosts previously cleared for this site are Grado, Stephen Purdon and Chris Toal. The photographs on this page do not label which person is which.

The set branding includes the show title, football jerseys, and the names Grado and Boab. That is the branding in the room, not a biography.

At the Scottish Podcast Awards '26 the show was tagged as Best Entertainment and Pop Culture Podcast. The hosts were not named in that post. This page does not add a winner's citation beyond that tag, and it does not add audience figures or episode counts.

The Daily Record reported the programme as a Scottish football podcast and reported that the hosts would record it at the Wishaw studio:

[Scottish TV stars to exclusively use Wishaw studio for their new podcast](https://www.dailyrecord.co.uk/in-your-area/lanarkshire/scottish-tv-stars-exclusively-use-27650370)
