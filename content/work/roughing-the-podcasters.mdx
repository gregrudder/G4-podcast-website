Roughing The Podcasters is an NFL podcast recorded with G4 in Wishaw. Permission has been given to name the show. Host names are not published on this page. Photographs are not on this page yet.

It sits with the other named shows recorded with G4. They are not accompanied by viewing or download figures.

The studios are in Wishaw, with free parking and multi-camera recording. The setup for a given episode is agreed at the booking. Hourly rates, and what each hour includes, are on the [pricing page](/pricing). Start with the [studio page](/podcast-studio) or the [contact form](/contact).
