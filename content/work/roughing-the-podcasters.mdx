Roughing The Podcasters is an NFL podcast recorded with G4 in Wishaw. Permission has been given to name the show. Host names are not published on this page.

It sits with the other two productions as a sport show recorded at the studios, alongside A Pint and Two Shots and the MMA show Leather’d. The three of them are the named work on this site. They are personality and sport programmes, not a set of business-podcast case studies, and they are not accompanied by viewing or download figures.

The studios themselves are in Wishaw, with free parking, multi-camera recording and both couch and desk sets. A TV can show a client’s branding. None of that is a claim about a specific camera plot for this NFL show. The setup for a given episode is agreed at the booking.

If you are recording a sport or hobby show with more than one person, the practical route is the same as for any other session: tell us how many people, whether you need video, and whether you need an edit. Hourly studio rates are published. The edit is quoted. Start with the [studio page](/podcast-studio) or the [contact form](/contact).
